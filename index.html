THE PERSON BEHIND PAA

I make a lot of different things.

Paintings. Writing. Conversations. Physical objects. Projects I haven't quite figured out yet.

I've never been particularly interested in choosing one medium and sticking with it. Usually, an idea comes first, and then I figure out what it needs. Sometimes that's paint. Sometimes it's a page. Sometimes it's a microphone. Sometimes it's learning an entirely new skill just to make something I've imagined.

The medium changes. I'm still just trying to bring the idea into the world.

And underneath all of it is faith. Not another category of what I do, but the foundation I'm doing it from.
